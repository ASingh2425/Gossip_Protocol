1. PRODUCT VISION & CORE PHILOSOPHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROBLEM STATEMENT:
"The Vanishing Dose — Detecting Medication Non-Adherence Without Asking"
A patient may be prescribed the right medication, yet treatment can fail because doses are missed, taken at the wrong time, or stopped altogether. Clinicians have very little visibility between appointments. Signals like refills, prescription changes, symptoms, wearable data, and follow-up records contain clues, but are rarely considered together.

CORE PHILOSOPHY:
"Don't just detect the vanishing dose. Explain where it vanished, remove the obstacle, and prove that it came back."

FOUNDATIONAL LAYER vs INNOVATION STACK:
- Base Layer (Original PS): Prescription -> Plan -> Reminders -> Tracking -> Adherence.
- Layer 1 (USP 1 - Execution Forensics): Reconstruct why a dose diverged using surrounding temporal evidence without asking the user.
- Layer 2 (USP 2 - Failure-Specific Access Recovery): Route detected access failures to verified nearby pharmacies with stock confidence ratings, price comparisons, and personalized excipient/drug safety checks.
- Layer 3 (USP 3 - Closed-Loop Verification): Verify that intervention actually restored physical stock (+30 tablets) and dose execution back to clinicians.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE 4-STAGE INTELLIGENCE MODEL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STAGE 1 — DETECT (Timeline Monitor):
- Monitors dose scheduled times passively.
- Flags execution divergence if reminder timeout occurs without confirmation.

STAGE 2 — EXPLAIN (Execution Forensics Engine):
- Analyzes 5 background signal vectors: Refill Overdue Days, Calculated Stock Depletion, Wearable Sleep/Timezone Shifts, Post-Dose Symptom Score, Schedule Delays.
- Generates Failure Attribution:
  * ACCESS_EXHAUSTION: Stock <= 0 at dose time, refill overdue. (High Certainty ~94%)
  * SIDE_EFFECT_AVOIDANCE: Post-dose symptom score >= 5/10 (Nausea/Epigastric distress). (Medium-High Certainty ~82%)
  * ROUTINE_DISRUPTION: Wearable travel timezone shift or severe sleep disruption. (Medium Certainty ~74%)
  * FORGETTING: Stock normal (>2 units), zero routine/symptom anomalies. (High Certainty ~90%)
- Computes dynamic Certainty Score (0-100%) and Confidence Level (HIGH_CONFIDENCE, MEDIUM_CONFIDENCE, LOW_CONFIDENCE).
- Categorizes Pattern Taxonomy (INTERMITTENT_SLIP, STRUCTURAL_DRIFT, ACUTE_ABANDONMENT).

STAGE 3 — RESOLVE (Medication Access Recovery Engine):
- Converts pharmacy search into a cause-specific recovery action.
- Availability Confidence Protocol:
  * HIGH_CONFIDENCE: Direct inventory API sync < 30 mins ago.
  * MEDIUM_CONFIDENCE: Partner confirmation < 6 hrs ago.
  * UNCONFIRMED: Stale data / manual listing.
- Personalized AI Alternative Brand & Excipient Safety Evaluator:
  * Evaluates candidate in-stock substitute brands (e.g. Stamlo 5mg, Amlopres 5mg, Amlokind-AT combo) against patient active co-medications and known allergies.
  * Outputs: 🟢 SAFE_EQUIVALENT (98% Score), 🟡 REQUIRES_PHARMACIST_CONSULT (68% Score), 🔴 CONTRAINDICATED_SUBSTITUTE (48% Score).
- 30-Minute Real-Time Stock Reservation Lock: Patient places a 30-min hold hold request on local pharmacy inventory while traveling.
- Pre-filled WhatsApp Generator: Auto-populates order text with drug name, brand, dosage, and delivery address.

STAGE 4 — VERIFY (Closed-Loop Verification Ledger):
- Tracks state machine: PENDING -> INTERVENTION_SENT -> STOCK_RESERVED -> MEDICATION_ACQUIRED -> DOSE_RESTORED -> VERIFIED_SUCCESS.
- Restores physical stock counter by +30 tablets upon acquisition.
- Updates Doctor Command Center triage status to GREEN.

4. FACTUAL CLINICAL DATASETS INTEGRATED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. NIH RxNorm / RxNav (U.S. National Library of Medicine):
   - Concept Unique Identifiers (RxCUI) for clinical drugs and semantic brand mapping.
   - Amlodipine 5mg Oral Tablet -> RxCUI: 197361
   - Atenolol 50mg Oral Tablet -> RxCUI: 197381
   - Metformin 500mg Oral Tablet -> RxCUI: 860975
   - Atorvastatin 10mg Oral Tablet -> RxCUI: 617314

2. FDA DailyMed Inactive Ingredient Database (IID & UNII Codes):
   - Unique Ingredient Identifiers for inactive binders, disintegrants, and allergens.
   - Lactose Monohydrate Binder -> UNII: EWQ57Q8I5X (Allergen flag: TRUE)
   - Microcrystalline Cellulose -> UNII: OP1R32D61U
   - Sodium Starch Glycolate -> UNII: H8AV0SQX4D

3. DrugBank 5.0 Clinical Drug Interaction Knowledge Graph (DDI):
   - Evaluates drug-drug and combination active ingredient interactions.
   - Amlodipine (DB00381) + Atenolol (DB00335) -> DB-INT-00381-00335 (Moderate hypotensive/bradycardic monitoring)
   - Atenolol (DB00335) + Metformin (DB00331) -> High severity warning (Beta-blockers mask hypoglycemia tachycardia symptoms - FDA DailyMed Warning 2.4).

4. PhysioNet MIMIC-IV Clinical Telemetry (MIT / Beth Israel):
   - Longitudinal Electronic Medication Administration Records (eMAR) used to establish baseline non-adherence curves.

7. REACTIVE STATE ENGINE & FUNCTIONAL FLOWS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STATE PROVIDER (`VanishingDoseContext.tsx`):
- `patients`: List of Patient objects.
- `pharmacies`: List of Pharmacy objects.
- `reservations`: List of active StockReservation objects.
- `selectedPatientId`: Currently active patient ID.

CORE CONTEXT METHODS:
1. `triggerRecoveryAction(patientId, nextStatus, details)`:
   - Advances patient `verificationStatus` to `nextStatus`.
   - If `nextStatus === 'VERIFIED_SUCCESS'` or `'MEDICATION_ACQUIRED'`, increases `supply.currentStock` by +30, updates missed doses to `RECOVERED`, and updates `riskStatus` to `GREEN`.
   - Appends entry to `patient.recoveryHistory`.
2. `verifyPharmacyStock(pharmacyId, inStock, priceINR)`:
   - Sets pharmacy `stockConfidence` to `HIGH_CONFIDENCE`.
   - Updates `lastVerifiedMinutesAgo` to `0`.
   - Sets `verificationSource` to `DIRECT_INVENTORY_API`.
3. `reservePharmacyStock(pharmacyId, patientId)`:
   - Creates a 30-minute `StockReservation` object.
   - Sets patient `verificationStatus` to `STOCK_RESERVED`.
   - Adds hold entry to pharmacy reservation queue.
4. `triggerAshaEscalation(patientId)`:
   - Sets `patient.ashaEscalated = true`.
   - Logs fallback alert dispatch in `recoveryHistory`.
5. `modifyRegimen(patientId, newDosage)`:
   - Updates `patient.supply.dosage` to `newDosage`.
   - Logs clinician adjustment in `recoveryHistory`.
